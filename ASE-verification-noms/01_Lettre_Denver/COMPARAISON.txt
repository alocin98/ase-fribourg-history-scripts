================ RÉPONSE ATTENDUE (HUMAINE) ================
Nov 17th 1913
Dr C.D. Spivak
Denver Colo.
Dear Friend
Your kind letter to hand
and contents worked. in reply wish
to thank you for the consideration
show My Brother Saul Fissman
will ask you to kindly advise as
To his condition. will afficeiate any favor
you may show us in this matter
With best wishes I am Sincerely yours
Anna Ferman.
Maplewood
MO

================ PRÉDICTION QWEN ================
Maplewood, Mo. Nov. 17th 193

Dr. C. S. Shaver
Denver Colo.

Dear Friend,

Your kind letter & hand and contents noted. In reply wish to thank you for the consideration shown my Brother Saul Lissman will ask you to kindly advise as to his condition. Will appreciate any favor you may show us in this matter.

With best wishes Sam. Sincerely yours

Anna Lerman.
Maplewood Mo
