Maplewood, Mo. Nov. 17th 193

Dr. C. S. Shaver
Denver Colo.

Dear Friend,

Your kind letter & hand and contents noted. In reply wish to thank you for the consideration shown my Brother Saul Lissman will ask you to kindly advise as to his condition. Will appreciate any favor you may show us in this matter.

With best wishes Sam. Sincerely yours

Anna Lerman.
Maplewood Mo

Enclosed please find stamped envelope
