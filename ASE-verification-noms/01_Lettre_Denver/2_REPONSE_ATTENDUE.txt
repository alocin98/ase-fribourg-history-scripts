Nov 17th 1913
Dr C.D. Spivak
Denver Colo.
Dear Friend
Your kind letter to hand
and contents worked. in reply wish
to thank you for the consideration
show My Brother Saul Fissman
will ask you to kindly advise as
To his condition. will afficeiate any favor
you may show us in this matter
With best wishes I am Sincerely yours
Anna Ferman.
Maplewood
MO
Enclosed please find stamped envelope
